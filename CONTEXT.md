# Scam Guardian

Scam Guardian supports a person making a transfer by distinguishing everyday use, difficulty, and possible coercion.

## Language

**Transfer session**: The period from opening the recipient step until a transfer is confirmed or cancelled. Browsing a wallet balance is outside the session.

**Normal**: A session consistent with the person's usual transfer activity, needing no intervention.

**Struggling**: A session showing difficulty navigating or correcting a transfer. It calls for help, not a fraud allegation.
_Avoid_: Suspicious senior, scam victim

**Coached**: A session with indicators that someone else may be directing the transfer. It is a risk classification, not proof of fraud.

**Simple mode**: A guided presentation with clearer instructions and larger controls for a struggling user.

**Cool-down**: A temporary pause before the person may choose to proceed or cancel a transfer.

**Trusted contact**: A person nominated by the user, with separate consent, to receive a minimal alert and contact them.
_Avoid_: Guardian administrator, payment approver

**Family alert**: A request for the trusted contact to call the user about a paused transfer. It excludes account details and amounts.

**Demo signal**: An explicitly simulated input used to demonstrate the transfer responses. It is distinct from an observed device capability.
