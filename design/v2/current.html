What Guardian never collects

The design works from a summary of the transfer session, kept on the device.

  • Call audio
  • Caller identity
  • Clipboard contents
  • Activity in other apps

One transfer at a time

Monitoring starts when you choose a recipient and ends when you confirm or cancel. Browsing your balance is outside it.

No amount. No account details. You choose the person, with separate consent.

A pause, never a block.

If a transfer looks coached, Guardian holds it for 30 seconds. Then Continue unlocks and the choice is yours. A canal lock works the same way: it holds a boat, then lets it through. Move your pointer across the gates.
